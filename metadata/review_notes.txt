The app opens Practice on a fresh install. Inspect the chart, reveal all candles using Replay, choose an answer, and check it to see feedback. Open Mark up to try drawing tools, then use Breakdown after a checked answer to compare markup with a reference. The Lessons and Progress tabs provide reading and attempt history. On later launches, the app opens Overview.

The course is in English. Chart prices and volume are synthetic. Progress and annotations are stored locally. No account, network connection, or protected-resource permissions are required. Small and medium widgets display a shared progress and chart snapshot and open Practice when tapped.
