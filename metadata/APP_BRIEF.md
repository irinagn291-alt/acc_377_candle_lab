# Candle Lab

## What it is

Candle Lab is an offline chart-reading practice app for people learning to interpret candlesticks, trends, price levels and volume. It combines short lessons with 24 exercises using synthetic charts, and tracks answers and progress on the device.

## Launch and onboarding

1. During loading, the app can show “Opening your lab…”.
2. On a first launch, it opens directly to the “Practice” tab. There is no separate onboarding sequence.
3. A banner says: “Your first 60 seconds: inspect a candle, then answer below. Every chart is a synthetic learning example.”
4. The first exercise is “Candle anatomy”, with all 36 candles initially visible. The user can inspect the chart and answer “Does the final candle have the widest high–low range?” using “Widest range” or “Not the widest”.
5. After an answer is checked, the app shows feedback and offers “Review chart” and “Try again”. On later launches, it opens “Overview” once an exercise has been selected or answered.

The tab bar contains “Overview”, “Practice”, “Lessons” and “Progress”.

## Screens

### Overview

The screen shows “CANDLE LAB”, “Learn to read the chart”, “A clearer view. One candle at a time.” and “Your personal chart-reading workspace.” The offline indicator is labelled “Course available offline”.

- “FOUNDATIONS” shows the number mastered out of 24 and the current exercise title. “Continue breakdown” opens the current exercise in “Practice”.
- “Training instruments” is marked “DEMO”. The three instrument rows show “EUR / USD”, “GBP / USD” and “USD / JPY”, each with “Synthetic · 36 candles”; tapping one opens its training exercise in “Practice”.
- Under “Build your eye”, each topic opens its first exercise in “Practice”: “Candle anatomy”, “Reading trends”, “Price levels”, “Candle structures”, “Volume & context” and “Limits of analysis”.
- The footer says “Candle Lab synthetic course · Dataset v1” and “Bundled examples. No live feed. Works entirely offline.”

### Practice

“PRACTICE LAB” shows the current topic. The “Lesson” selector, accessible as “Choose lesson”, lists all 24 numbered exercises by topic and variant; choosing one opens it on this screen. The instrument selector offers “EUR / USD”, “GBP / USD” and “USD / JPY” and selects an exercise for that instrument within the current topic.

The chart shows “TRAINING DATA”, “1H SOURCE”, a date, open/high/low/close values labelled “O”, “H”, “L” and “C”, candlesticks and volume. “1H”, “3H” and “6H” change the displayed candle interval. For an aggregated interval, the exercise says “Chart aggregated to 3H. Answers use the full 1H source section.” or “Chart aggregated to 6H. Answers use the full 1H source section.”

- “Cursor” selects a candle; “Pan” lets the user zoom and move around the chart. The “Candle … / …” stepper also selects a candle.
- “Mark up” opens the drawing screen.
- “Restart replay” returns to six revealed candles. “Play replay” reveals them over time and changes to “Pause replay”. “Step” reveals one more candle; “All”, accessible as “Reveal all candles”, reveals all 36. The counter shows how many are visible.
- The exercise panel is labelled “OBSERVE & EXPLAIN”. Its prompts and answer controls depend on the topic:
  - “Does the final candle have the widest high–low range?”: “Widest range” or “Not the widest”.
  - “How do closing prices move across this section?”: “Upward”, “Downward” or “Sideways”.
  - “Place a horizontal level at the highest wick in the full section.”: add a “Level” in “Mark up”, then use “Check my level”.
  - “Does the final candle meet our doji criterion?”: “Doji” or “Regular body”.
  - “Compare the first six and last six volume bars.”: “Activity increased” or “Activity decreased”.
  - “Which statement is supported by this section?”: “Closes rose overall”, “Closes fell overall” or “The next candle is certain”.
- A checked answer displays “Criterion met” or “Let’s look again”, followed by an explanation. “Review chart” opens “Breakdown”; “Try again” clears the feedback so the user can answer again.

### Mark up

“Mark up” shows the instrument, “Synthetic training” and a full chart. The tools are “Cursor”, “Pan”, “Level”, “Trend line” and “Zone”. The screen gives instructions for the selected tool: select a candle, zoom and pan, place a horizontal level, connect two observations, or highlight a price zone.

“Selected candle: …” changes the selected candle. “Level at selected high” places a horizontal level at that candle’s high. “Undo” reverses a drawing change or removes the last saved annotation. The screen shows the count as “… saved annotations”, with “Saved on this device” or “Save needs attention”, and lists each annotation’s type and values. “Done” returns to “Practice”.

### Breakdown

“Breakdown” opens after “Review chart”. It shows “See the reasoning.”, the synthetic chart and a “Chart overlay” choice between “My markup” and “Breakdown”. The latter shows the reference overlay; the former shows the user’s annotations.

“The criterion” contains the exercise explanation, followed by “A useful description has a boundary.” and an explanation that other time frames and definitions can produce different interpretations. The source is identified as “Source: Candle Lab synthetic course, dataset v1. Fixed educational examples; no market update time applies.”

“Done” returns to the current exercise. “Next exercise” closes the breakdown and opens the next exercise in “Practice”; after exercise 24 it returns to exercise 1.

### Lessons

“The library” shows “Less guessing. More understanding.” and “Six short reads. Twenty-four ways to practise.” “Search lessons” filters the topic list by title or subtitle; “Clear search” clears the query.

Each topic opens a short reading:

- “Candle anatomy” — “Read the body. Notice the wick.”
- “Reading trends” — “See the sequence, not one candle.”
- “Price levels” — “Map the extremes of a section.”
- “Candle structures” — “Give a shape its proper context.”
- “Volume & context” — “Add participation to the picture.”
- “Limits of analysis” — “Describe evidence, not certainty.”

A topic shows “3 min · 4 exercises” until marked read, then “Read · 4 exercises”. In a reading, “Mark as read” records it as read and changes to “Read ✓”. “Practise this topic” opens its first exercise in “Practice”. The navigation back control returns to “The library”.

### Progress

“Your progress” shows “Understanding, made visible.” and three statistics: “Accuracy”, “Mastered” and “Read”. “Accuracy by topic” shows a percentage or “Not tried” for each topic.

“Practise …” opens the first exercise of the weakest attempted topic; before any attempts it offers “Practise candle anatomy”. “Recent mistakes” lists up to the 12 most recent incorrect answers. Tapping one reopens that exercise in “Practice”.

The screen explains: “Accuracy includes all attempts. Mastery counts each correctly completed exercise once. Your progress stays on this device.”

### Home Screen widget

The small and medium “Practice lab” widget is described as “Your current synthetic chart and learning progress.” It can show the current topic, the number mastered out of 24, “lessons mastered”, the current lesson and exercise, and “Synthetic charts · for learning”. Tapping it opens “Practice”.

## Features

- Six short lessons and 24 synthetic chart exercises.
- “Cursor” and “Pan” chart interaction, with “1H”, “3H” and “6H” intervals.
- Candle replay with “Restart replay”, “Play replay”, “Pause replay”, “Step” and “All”.
- “Mark up” with “Level”, “Trend line”, “Zone” and “Undo”.
- Answer feedback and a “Breakdown” reference overlay.
- Lesson search, read markers, accuracy, mastery and recent mistakes.
- “Practice lab” Home Screen widget.
- Offline synthetic training examples; no live feed.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- After replay is restarted, the answers are replaced by “Reveal all 36 candles before checking this section.” Use “Play replay”, “Step” or “All” to reveal the rest.
- “Step” is disabled when all 36 candles are visible. “Restart replay” starts again from six; playing from the end also restarts from six.
- “Check my level” is disabled until at least one “Level” has been added in “Mark up”. Place a level near the highest wick, tap “Done”, then check it. The displayed tolerance determines whether it meets the criterion.
- “Undo” is disabled when there is nothing to undo or remove.
- “My markup” in “Breakdown” can show “No annotations yet. Add your observations in Mark up.” Return to “Practice” and add one to see it there.
- A search with no results shows “No matching lessons” and “Try candles, trends, levels or volume.” Change the search or use “Clear search”.
- Before an answer, “Accuracy” is “—”, topics show “Not tried”, and “Recent mistakes” says “Your practice story starts here.” After attempts with no mistakes, it says “No mistakes to revisit yet.” Incorrect answers appear as they are recorded; only the latest 12 are shown.
- “Next exercise” wraps from the last exercise back to the first. Repeated answers count as separate attempts, while each correctly completed exercise counts once toward “Mastered”.
- If saved progress cannot be opened, the app says “Your saved progress could not be opened. Retry, or reset the local save to start again.” It offers “Retry” and “Reset local save”; reset asks “Reset your saved progress and drawings?” and starts over.
- If saving fails, the app says “Progress could not be saved. Your current session is still available. Free some storage and retry.” Use “Retry” after freeing storage.

## Starter content and resume

The app starts with six readings and 24 fixed synthetic exercises, each containing 36 candles. The first exercise is already selected and fully revealed; no prior answers or annotations are required.

The current exercise, revealed-candle count, annotations, answers and read markers are saved on the device, so work can be resumed. Answer feedback itself is not retained as an open result after relaunch.

## Permissions

None.

## Absent

Login or accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, an account deletion flow and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are absent.

User-created chart annotations are present. There is no sharing or publishing flow for them.

## Data and support

Progress, answers, read markers, replay position and drawings stay on this device. The course works offline. There is no on-screen Contact or Support control.

## Scanning and health

None. The app does not scan barcodes or QR codes and does not show health, medical or product-health information.

## Platform

The interface uses English, a British English locale and left-to-right layout regardless of region. It supports iPhone and iPad on iOS 17.0 or later. iPhone supports portrait and both landscape orientations; iPad also supports upside-down portrait.
